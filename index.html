Creating Games That Connect People & Build Memories

Owning the Strategy. Leading the People. Shipping the Vision.

There was always someone who had to sit out and wait. I noticed that when I was very young, could not stop thinking about it, and have been doing something about it ever since. Every game I have worked on and every team I have led comes back to that same thing: pull everyone in together and make something worth remembering.

I spent five years building in Unity before stepping into production full time, working across level design, systems, shaders, narrative, and full pipeline work. That background matters: when I am running a sprint I know what the developer across from me is actually dealing with. At Gamemano I built the production pipeline from nothing, led a 43-person cross-functional team across developers, artists, game mathematicians, QA, legal, and external sound vendors, and shipped 13 game titles across PC, WebGL, and mobile in three months. I have always led from the same place. Football team captain in school, in-game leader for my Counter-Strike squad, captain of a mixed-rank Valorant team (Bronze to Gold players) that placed 64th in the APAC qualifiers, now producing games. The same instincts that make a good team captain make a good producer.

Right now I am producing Planet of Twins, an original PC action-adventure game built around two characters who have to stay together to survive. It is where every part of my background meets. I own the roadmap, run the sprints, do the playtesting, and track the KPIs end to end with a two-person team. I am the producer, the developer, and the player who cares about whether the game actually feels right.

I hold my CAPM from PMI and am working toward PMP. Game Producer, Product Owner, Project Manager, Game Designer. What I have learned makes a project actually ship comes down to three things: leadership, open communication, and problem solving. The last one is wired into how I think, and you can see it in the systems I build.

When I am not working I am playing Valorant, Rust, or Schedule 1 with friends, or somewhere on a football pitch. The full story is on the About Me page.
