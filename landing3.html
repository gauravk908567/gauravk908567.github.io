In short

What it is, and what I did on it

  • The game

    Inspired by Special Cybernetic Attack Team and Meteoride. Enemies come in types with their own behaviour, and the difficulty climbs the longer you last.

  • My part

    Design, code, art and animation.

  • What it gave me

    The fundamentals: movement, enemy behaviour, spawning, and difficulty that scales over time.

Hands-on

What I built

  • Movement and controls. Smooth, responsive movement through the level.

  • Enemy behaviour. Several enemy types, each with its own way of attacking.

  • Bullet spawning. A spawning system that keeps combat unpredictable.

  • Rising difficulty. The game gets harder over time, so it keeps asking more of the player.

  • Endless scrolling. A parallax background that gives the scene depth.

  • Animated menu. A main menu with objects drifting at random.

How it was built

  • Movement, enemy behaviour and bullet spawning on Unity's 2D physics, with my own scripts on top
  • Every gameplay element and every menu screen animated inside Unity
  • Git for version control, even working alone

The look

Simple, hand-made art in Illustrator and Photoshop, kept close to the retro look of the shooters it is inspired by.
