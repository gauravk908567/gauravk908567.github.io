In short

What it is, and what I did on it

  • The game

    The togetherness of Bomberman in a fast first-person shooter: get into a room with friends and play.

  • My part

    All of it, with the networking as the main subject.

  • What it gave me

    Networking fundamentals: lobbies, matchmaking and keeping every player in sync.

Hands-on

What I built

  • Lobby and rooms. Players can create, find, join and leave rooms.

  • Error handling. When creating a room fails, the menu says so clearly and offers the next step.

  • Random levels. Each match picks from a set of level templates with a Fisher-Yates shuffle.

  • Synced controls. Player controls synchronised across the server, with callbacks that reflect changes in real time.

  • Items. A grenade, the first item in the game, with more planned.
