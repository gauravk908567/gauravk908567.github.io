In short

What it is, and what I did on it

  • The game

    Climb through platforms that each behave differently, with spikes above to keep the pressure on. High scores are saved between sessions.

  • My part

    Design, code, art and publishing. I made the assets myself and shipped both the Android and the WebGL build.

  • Why it matters to me

    It was the first time I took a game all the way to a store page.

Hands-on

What I built

Three kinds of platform

One breaks after you land on it. One pushes you in the direction it is marked with. One follows your movement, and can trick you into the spikes.

  • Endless scrolling. A parallax background on a quad, so the climb never ends.

  • Saved scores. High scores kept between sessions with PlayerPrefs.

  • Publishing. Released an Android build on Google Play and a WebGL build on itch.io.

  • Version control. Git from the start, so earlier versions were always there to go back to.

How it was built

  • Character movement and the platforms run on Unity's 2D physics, with my own scripts on top
  • The main menu uses Unity's UI buttons and leads straight into the game
  • High scores and game state are kept with PlayerPrefs

What it taught me

It was my first WebGL build, and my first time through the whole route from a project to two stores: the Android build for Google Play and the WebGL build for itch.io.
